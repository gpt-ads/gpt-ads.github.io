# Paid Media Notes

A practitioner's read on gpt ads: the pilot timeline, the tiers that see them, the console you get after signup, and the numbers OpenAI has not published.

**Read the full page:** https://gpt-ads.github.io/

If you buy media for a consumer brand and your customers research before they buy, this inventory is worth a test budget, because the pilot has already moved from one country to nine and the entry offer refunds a chunk of the first spend. It is not worth a test if you sell to developers or to enterprise buyers, since the ad-supported tiers are Free and Go, and Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education accounts see nothing. The one caveat is measurement: the console reports impressions, clicks and conversions on your own campaigns, but there is no public library that shows what anyone else is running. For that side of the job, Reachara tracks competitor ads and the creators behind them.
