Where gpt ads actually appear

The placement sits inside a ChatGPT conversation, below the assistant's answer rather than woven into it. OpenAI's own framing is that people arrive to explore options, compare choices and make decisions, and the ad is meant to land at that moment of intent rather than against a keyword. Three commitments are repeated on the advertiser pages: ads are clearly identified in the experience, they stay distinct from the response, and they do not influence what ChatGPT says. Conversations are kept private from advertisers, which is the part media buyers ask about first. The example on the advertiser home page is a person planning a backyard fence and a cedar privacy panel ad from a supply company, which tells you the shape of the format better than any spec sheet: a single relevant product, shown once the need is obvious.

Pilot timeline, country by country

OpenAI began testing on 9 February 2026 in the United States, limited to logged-in adult users on the Free and Go tiers. Paid tiers were excluded from the start and still are. On 26 March the company said early results were encouraging, citing no measurable impact on consumer trust metrics and low dismissal rates, and announced pilots in Canada, Australia and New Zealand. On 11 August the program launched in the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea, with a stated intention to keep expanding through the year. That is nine markets in six months, which matters for planning: if your category is seasonal, the inventory in your country may be newer than your campaign calendar assumes. Check the announcement page before you budget, because the list has changed roughly every quarter.

Inside Ads Manager after signup

Signing up at the advertiser site drops you into Ads Manager, and the structure will feel ordinary to anyone who has used a search console. You create an account, then campaigns with a budget and a goal, then ad groups, then the ads themselves. Ad details can be typed in directly or bulk uploaded as CSV, which is the detail that decides whether a large catalogue is practical to run. Reporting covers impressions, clicks and conversions with monthly trend charts and summary metrics. A Help Center sits alongside the tool with step-by-step resources. What you will not find on the public pages is a rate card: no CPM, no minimum spend, no auction mechanics. The signup flow and a sales contact form are the only ways to get those numbers.

Who is already buying

The advertiser site names Newegg, Best Buy, Lowe's and VistaPrint among early advertisers, which is a useful signal about fit. Every one of them sells considered purchases: electronics, appliances, home improvement, print. Alec Shao, a performance marketing manager at Newegg, describes the goal as being part of the conversation while someone researches what to buy, and building consideration at that moment. Read that as an upper-funnel buy with a conversion tail, not a last-click channel. There is a starting offer of 500 dollars in ad credit for new advertisers who spend 500 dollars, which effectively halves the cost of a first structured test. If your category has a long comparison phase, that test is cheap enough to be worth running before the inventory gets crowded.

The blind spot nobody mentions

Search has an ads transparency library. Social platforms have ad libraries. Conversational inventory, so far, has none, which means you can see how your own campaigns perform and nothing at all about how competitors are positioning against you. That gap is fine for a test budget and painful once the channel matters. The workaround is to keep watching the channels where competitor creative is visible and to treat the messaging you find there as a proxy for what rivals will say in a conversation. Running a first test without wasting the credit

  1. Confirm your market is live
    Read the announcement page before anything else. Nine countries are live and the list changes; a campaign built for a market that has not launched is a wasted week.
  2. Sign up and claim the credit
    Create the advertiser account, then claim the 500 dollar offer at signup rather than after your first invoice. Ads Manager opens straight after the account is created.
  3. Build one campaign, one goal
    Set a single budget and a single goal, then split creative across ad groups. Bulk CSV upload handles a catalogue; type the ads in directly if you only have a handful.
  4. Judge it on research intent
    Look at conversions with a longer window than you would use for search. The stated value is consideration while someone compares options, not an immediate click-to-cart.

Questions people actually ask

Do gpt ads change the answers ChatGPT gives?

OpenAI states that ads do not influence answers, that they stay visually distinct from the response, and that they are clearly identified as ads. The stated purpose of the format is to support broader free access rather than to shape recommendations, and the company frames answer independence as one of its core pilot principles.

Which subscribers see advertising?

Logged-in adult users on the Free and Go tiers. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education plans were excluded when the test started in February 2026 and remain excluded in the updates published since. If your buyers mostly sit on paid tiers, this channel will not reach them.

What does it cost to advertise?

No rate card appears on the public advertiser pages, so anyone quoting a CPM for this inventory is guessing. What is documented is a starting offer of 500 dollars in ad credit after 500 dollars of spend. For actual pricing you have to sign up or use the sales request form.

Can advertisers see my conversations?

No. The published principles say conversations stay private from advertisers and that people keep control over their experience. Targeting is described as using richer context signals inside the product rather than handing transcripts to buyers, and the policy pages are the place to verify the current wording.

Is there an ad library for this channel?

Not on any page crawled for this review. You can measure your own campaigns in Ads Manager, but there is no public archive of competitor creative the way search and social platforms offer. Tools such as Reachara cover competitor ad monitoring on the channels that are visible.

Is it too early to bother?

That depends on your category. Considered purchases, the kind people research for days, are exactly what the early advertisers sell: Newegg, Best Buy, Lowe's and VistaPrint. Impulse categories have less to gain right now, and the credit offer makes waiting cheaper than a badly targeted test.
